description: >-
  As the season turns and the world quiets, we invite you to an experience with
  music, stillness, and warmth in the serene setting of Eric Ericsonhallen.


  Let go of the holiday hustle, meditate, or simply listen as the atmosphere
  wraps around you.


  performances by MARIAM WALLENTIN // LUMEN VOICES // HAGA VOKALENSEMBLE // LEO
  SVENSSON & JOHAN ARRIAS


  Mariam Wallentin is an artist who moves us to feel and reflect. With roots in
  improvisation, jazz, and the sounds of pop and soul, Mariam’s career has
  traversed diverse artistic landscapes. She recently released a solo album
  called Breathing Techniques and is also a core member of Wildbirds &
  Peacedrums as well as a driving force in the powerhouse jazz collective Fire!
  Orchestra. Wallentin continually pushes new musical boundaries.


  Lumen Voices, with artistic director Katarina Henryson, is a flexible voice
  ensemble in size and style, that works with Improvisation and new music across
  and between genres.


  Haga Vokalensemble is a choir based in Stockholm that consists of 13 singers.
  The choir was founded in 2020 by conductor Anton Alexis Hallgren.


  Leo Svensson Sander , composer and cellist, spans classical, Scandinavian
  folk, and experimental music. He founded the avant-pop band The Tiny and has
  contributed to numerous albums and films, focusing increasingly on theater,
  film, and dance composition. Since 2019, he has performed with jazz harpist
  Stina Hellberg Agback in the duo Namna.


  Johan Arrias, a Stockholm-based musician and composer, specializes in
  experimental and improvised music with saxophone and clarinet. He blends
  instrumental works, field recordings, electronics, and improvisation,
  emphasizing sound, place, and collaboration across various creative contexts.


  This event is a special collaboration with Eric Ericson International Choral
  Centre.
